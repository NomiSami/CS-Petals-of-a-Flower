# Mimiga Lore

## Canon Basics
- White-furred rabbit-dog, long ears, eat flowers + fish, live in moment, grieve sharply but briefly, focus on immediate (food, day's catch)
- Speech: short, plain, earnest, slightly broken translation-like, "Oh." "...", deadpan humor, present-focused
- Culture: songs (Noriko sisters 3 lines), lanterns, Assembly Hall fireplace, burial in graveyard, humble numbered titles (number one leader, number two guard), brass house keys generations-old
- Numbers: Cradle 6 core + few dozen holdouts in Old Village

## Fanfic Additions
- **Origin myth:** Island was cold lonely rock, seed fell, grew white 5-petal flower, petals fell into caves woke Mimigas "Oh. Hello. There you are." Flower didn't die became them — every Mimiga is petal of same flower, scattered far forgetful but same flower. Told by Arthur to Toroko.
- **Diaspora:** After Mugen + Doctor, scattered to Bellwater (largest refugee), Windside, Terraces, Grasstown
- **Food economy:** Meal flowers now staple, once garnish. Yamashita Farm tends since before Cradle. Never red in Yamashita bed (taboo rhyme)
- **Soul flower culture:** Flower-keepers (Ume) hold ritual, pairbloom courtship giggled, sorrowcup grief, dreambell funeral dream-walking, red taboo
- **Naming:** Fish/food/plant-based: Kanpachi, Hamachi, Sandaime (third gen Yamashita), Santa, Chaco, Mahin, etc.

## The Flower's Effect on Mimigas
- **Red:** rage/strength/size, cooked = less rage but craving, King's eyes red since winter, Arthur pink-rimmed before sealing, hands burnt-honey smell from cooking
- **Dreambell:** visit dead, risk lost
- **Sorrowcup:** numb grief, addictive
- **Pairbloom:** aphrodisiac pairing rite
- **Lullaby:** cure, restores reason, scent, rare
