# Worldbuilding — Island & Beyond

## The Island
- **Size:** Dozens miles across, floating island held aloft by Core in Labyrinth deep. Upper surface meadows/cliffs, below honeycomb biomes: bushland caverns, sand halls, lightless lakes, fungal groves, wind-scoured rim Outer Wall. Underground networks vast, only partly mapped by Stonemoles.
- **The Cradle:** Game's village, pinched into bowl of rock. Lit by **Crack**, seam far overhead where daylight enters. Morning = when light hits Reservoir. Yamashita beds use tilted polished slabs to throw light. Contains: Arthur's House (blue door, teleporter EGG CORRIDOR STANDBY), Assembly Hall (fireplace hides Bubbline), Shack (half-collapsed storeroom, Toroko hiding spot), Reservoir (dark crack at bottom, Old Lord legend), Yamashita Farm (meal flowers, never red), Graveyard gate (Jack's post), Cage.
- **Old Village / Big Village:** Miles of overgrown lanes, intact empty homes, Terraces, Bell Hall, Old Square, Burrows, Lantern Row, edge-houses where lamps went out first. Still crossroads for traders/gatherers. Few holdouts refuse to leave. Someone lights lamps.
- **Map sketch:** First Cave (Start Point, Hermit Gunsmith Polar Star) above Cradle, Reservoir bottom, Farm middle, Assembly Hall top, Graveyard above that, Old Village sprawls beyond rim.

## Technology
- **Teleporters:** Ancient builder tech, predates Mimigas, left by unknown builders/war-era. Booster rewired some with plain English labels. Arthur's house has one.
- **Robots:** Quote, Curly sent 10y ago to destroy crown, defeated bearer but lost. Now feared by Mimigas, Zett says not all evil.
- **Weapons:** Polar Star with Gunsmith, King's Blade = Arthur's sword Jack brought home after Arthur vanished, Fireball from Santa.

## Flora — Meal vs Soul
### Meal Flowers (sustenance)
Fast/hardy, few calories, once garnish beside veg/fruit/fish/meat, now staple + Kanpachi fish.

| Flower | Color | Taste/Use |
|---|---|---|
| Sweetcups | yellow | sweet, bunched like forgetful bells |
| Saltbells | blue | salty, scarce salt underground |
| Crunchstars | white | crisp, star-shaped |
| Pepperlace | orange | hot |
| Lampweed | violet | pressed for lamp oil, edible pinch |

### Soul Flowers (marked by dark soul-line down petal, dedicated ritual: darkness, timing, water, songs)
- **Red flower:** strength, size, rage, loss of reason. Cooking dulls rage not craving. Every red is soul flower. Taboo.
- **Dreambell:** visions, dream-walking, funeral rites to "visit" dead. Some never return. Used to enter Mugen dream.
- **Sorrowcup:** numbs grief, habit-forming, common in refugee towns (Bellwater)
- **Pairbloom:** pairing rites aphrodisiac, talked in giggles never front children, never grown accidentally
- **Lullaby:** white bell cure, costly, scent restores reason, needs rare conditions, Ume holds knowledge, spreads on wind

Children's rhyme: *Sweet is yellow, salt is blue. Lined ones aren't for you. Red ones — never, never, never.*

Arthur secretly cultivated hidden stash red flowers preparing for Mugen. King has been eating from stash — ticking clock, withdrawal.

## Magic / Red Flowers / Gem
- **Demon Crown:** Tall metal, single red Eye. Eye = gem = Red Crystal. Lost when crown broken in war. Doctor's current crown has empty socket or crude synthetic red resin lump, touches it when alone.
- **Eye powers:** Possession, control frenzied minds, bypasses human immunity. Mugen with Eye alone could transform Mimigas directly. Doctor with crown but no Eye can only command Misery/Balrog, hence hunting Eye.
- **Mugen:** Dream-being born Ballos leaking dreams, lonely envies Mimiga warmth, lures isolated/grieving, calls victims "petals" (chilling double meaning). Touch with gem -> body hollow Sleeper (grey fur, red glass eyes, wander wordlessly), soul to endless dream. When sealed, link broke, Picked collapsed into unending sleep where stood. Centralized in Hall of Sleepers tended secretly by Hozuki. If seal breaks, rise.

## Settlements
- **Bellwater:** Lake town under hanging stone "bell", biggest Mimiga town left, wary elder council (Grandmother Shiso), refuses aid at first
- **Windside:** Cliff homes on rim, wind never stops, Hoppies, Little family beneath wall
- **Flower Terraces:** Sacred origin soul flower cultivation, half-abandoned, Ume last keeper, corrupted into Plantation via stolen techniques
- **Grasstown:** Jellyfish took back, Chaco (flirty outsider, lipstick, Jellyfish Juice fireplace, Arthur connection), Santa (cowardly, lost key, Fireball), Cthulhu hooded wanderers, Shelter with Kazuma
- **Sand Zone:** Colons tiny, Curly amnesiac protects them, Jenka old witch, 5 puppies, storehouse red seeds
- **Plantation:** Doctor's red flower operation enslaved Mimigas, Shovel Brigade collaborators who believe Doctor protects them vs Zett/Chie pushback, jails, Momorin hideout backwards password, Numahachi statues, Rest Area
- **Deep:** Longears/Deepfolk = war frenzied who went deep not surface, feral then mellowed, distinct culture, remember lullaby
