# Protagonists

## Toroko — POV Core
- Species: Mimiga, Arthur's younger sister
- Age: child, "nearly grown" (avoid canon number)
- Appearance: white fur, long ears, pink dress, brass house key on cord
- Origin: Cradle, Arthur's house blue door bottom of bowl
- Motivation: Keep Arthur's house, keep promise, protect Sue, find why brother didn't return
- Flaw: Impulsive, lives in moment, refuses to dwell on quiet
- Arc: From child who needs flower story to one who tells it, carries Arthur's legacy
- Relation: Arthur sister, King friend, Sue shelterer
- Voice sample: "I'm not a kid! I'm nearly grown!" / "Soup?"

## Sue Sakamoto — POV Core, Outsider Bridge
- Species: Human turned Mimiga
- Appearance: Human: green ponytail, blue sweater, dark leggings, silver locket photo Mom/Kazuma/self. Mimiga: white-grey fur, same locket, torn blue sweater
- Origin: Surface, research expedition with Mom Momorin, brother Kazuma, Booster, Itoh, Doctor
- Motivation: Find Kazuma + Mom, understand transformation, get home, then choose stay
- Flaw: Prickly, hates Mimigas as mirror/threat, calls village "hole", judges passivity unfairly
- Arc: Disdain -> belonging, surface girl scientific literate through mother, brave but scared, learns Mimiga reasons for staying
- Relation: Toroko sheltered by, King distrusts, Kanpachi rescued
- Weapon: Knowledge of teleporters, surface tech
- Voice: Modern sardonic vs Mimiga simple. "I'm not a Mimiga!"

## King — POV Core, Burdened Leader
- Species: Mimiga
- Appearance: Massive broad-shouldered, biggest in Cradle maybe, pink scar muzzle, red sleepless eyes since winter, Arthur's old blade crossways back
- Origin: Cradle, number one after Arthur
- Motivation: Keep count at six not seven, protect what left, don't lose anyone else
- Flaw: Volatile temper, secret cooked red flower addiction, distrustful, carries Arthur's sword Jack brought home
- Arc: Addiction ticking clock, follows Arthur's footsteps searching safe dose, must choose raw flower to save Toroko (Storehouse), survives scarred fraying, final stand
- Relation: Arthur friend, Toroko protector, Jack number two
- Voice sample: "There are only six of us left." / "Good."

## Jack — POV Core, Historian Guard
- Species: Mimiga
- Appearance: Thin, staff, pack, lantern, ears up
- Origin: Cradle, number two
- Motivation: Guard graveyard (seal), keep Arthur story, protect Toroko
- Flaw: Keeps secrets (empty grave, seal), alone
- Arc: From guard who says "graveyard's taken over by mushrooms" to one who shows what he's been keeping lit (Hall of Sleepers)
- Relation: Arthur went down with, returned alone, King secret-share
- Role: Tells Arthur + Red Demon story in Assembly Hall

## Supporting POVs
### Kanpachi
- Quiet perceptive, counts days by catches, Reservoir dock, second stool empty for brother Hamachi, tackle box. Haunted by missing Hamachi (Igor). Old Lord fish legend. Finds Sue + green hair.

### Mahin
- Soft-voiced droopy-eared, always eating sweetcups, round belly, gentle, eats alone chapter. Night watch outside Arthur's house munching quietly.

### Sandaime
- Third gen Yamashita Farm, meal flower tender, grumbles, saltbells.

### Hozuki (Original)
- Old Village lamplighter, eccentric, wise elder, lights lamps in empty houses where Sleepers lie so they won't wake in darkness. Lights every lantern as signal in Bloom.

### Yomogi (Original)
- Young trader woman, carries news between settlements on pack-beetle Mochi, beetle-back merchant.

### Ume (Original)
- Last flower-keeper, Flower Terraces, holds soul flower ritual knowledge, white Lullaby cure.

- Quote: silent, Mimigas read nods incorrectly, charming
