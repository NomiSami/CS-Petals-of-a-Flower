- Why oppose: Cold utilitarian "convenient" view of village, charismatic grandiose, manipulates Plantation Mimigas framing robots as enemy protector
- Moral grayness: Was kind, unchanging smile worst detail, genuinely believes?

- Boss: Throne Room Balcony

## Third-Party — Mugen (Original, Yours)
- Nature: Ancient lonely dream-being born Ballos leaking dreams, gentle seductive, envies Mimiga warmth, lures isolated grieving
- Name: 夢幻 dream/illusion + 無限 without limit
- Goal: Wants gem back, calls transformed victims "petals" — chilling double meaning vs novel title, contrast plucked bouquet vs belonging flower, echoes Doctor's picking
- Method: Touch with gem -> hollow Sleepers (grey fur red glass eyes wander wordlessly to old homes at night) + soul to endless dream. Picked off edge-houses one by one
- Sealing: Arthur descended via graveyard caves with prepared soul flowers, dream-state, bound using own soul as anchor, body dormant in seal chamber neither dead nor alive, blade left behind. Gem sealed alongside. Only Jack/King know, needs Arthur bloodline Toroko to open
- Stakes: If seal breaks link reforms Sleepers rise
- Twist: Maybe Arthur became Red Demon himself after eating red flowers to seal, fled to depths, empty grave, fan theory recontextualized
