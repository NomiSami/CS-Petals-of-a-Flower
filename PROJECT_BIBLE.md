# Project Bible — Petals of a Flower

> Source of truth. All high-end model outputs must respect this. Compiled from Gist https://gist.github.com/NomiSami/5dc16e7e75612fc3cb2d609005830208

## Core Premise
A year after Arthur's disappearance, Mimiga Village has collapsed from ~1000 to a few dozen. The Cradle — the game's 6-Mimiga camp — is all that's left of a vast, empty Old Village. When Sue Sakamoto, human-turned-Mimiga, falls into the Reservoir, Toroko shelters her in Arthur's house. The Doctor wants Sue back, but he also wants what's sealed beneath the graveyard: Mugen, and its gem — the Demon Crown's missing Eye.

This is Mimiga-centric Cave Story: Quote sleeps through the opening the player remembers, so King, Jack, Toroko, and Sue must do what he did, and more.

## Shape
- **Length:** ~150,000 words / 480-500 pages
- **Structure:** 5 Petals x 8 chapters + Prologue + Epilogue = 42 sections, ~3,500 words avg
- **Delivery:** 2-3 chapters per installment (~8-11k words), ~16 installments
- **POV:** Rotating close third person, one POV per chapter. Main: Toroko, Sue, King, Jack. Occasional: Kanpachi, Mahin, Sandaime, Hozuki, Balrog (once).

- **The Six:** King (number one), Jack (number two, guards mushroom-overrun graveyard), Toroko, Mahin (always eating), Kanpachi (fishes Reservoir, says Sue fell in), Sandaime (Yamashita Farm)
- **Arthur:** Warrior, former leader, Toroko's older brother, buried in graveyard with spare key at grave, red petals in corner of his house ("...Flowers?"), Jack's story about Arthur and Red Demon
- **King's eyes:** Red from red flowers. Pixel BBS: "by red flower, for the power, He may have cooked it"
- **Sue:** Arrived with Momorin, Kazuma, Booster, Itoh, Doctor. Turned into Mimiga (Misery did it, Doctor watched). Prickly except to Toroko. Gave Toroko silver locket. Sheltered in Arthur's house, Toroko won't give King the key
- **Demon Crown:** Tall metal, single red eye. Misery and Balrog cursed to serve wearer. Ballos sealed by sister Jenka is source of power
- **War 10 years ago:** Robots came for crown, slaughtered Mimigas. Cornered Mimigas ate red flowers, fought back, vanished rumored to surface. Jenka's speech is canon
- **Others:** Chaco + "that wimp Santa", Cthulhu "Isn't the war over? Oh well.", Curly + Colons, Jenka's 5 puppies (Hajime, Kakeru, Mick, Nene, Shinobu), Zett/Chie/Numahachi/Shovel Brigade, Ma Pignon, backwards password

## Divergences (Intentional)
- **Soldier sleeps:** Quote at Start Point, not awake for opening. Mimigas must act. Wakes late Petal 2 as cliffhanger, companion in Petal 3, silent — Mimigas interpret nods
- **Village split:** Cradle (game's camp: farm, Reservoir, Assembly Hall, Shack, Arthur's house, graveyard gate) + Old Village / Big Village (miles of overgrown lanes, empty intact homes, Terraces, Bell Hall, Old Square). King counts 6, true is few dozen plus holdouts
- **Population:** Post-war influx to ~1000, then Mugen emptied edge-houses, then Doctor's kidnappings emptied rest. Diaspora to Bellwater, Windside, Terraces, Grasstown
- **Mugen + Gem:** Gem = Demon Crown's missing red Eye = Red Crystal. Lost when crown broken in war. Found by Mugen, used to "pick" Mimigas. Sealed by Arthur beneath graveyard with gem. Doctor's current crown has empty socket (or crude synthetic red resin), hence he touches it when alone. He wants true Eye to complete control
- **Flower lore expanded:** Meal vs Soul flowers (see lore/worldbuilding.md)
- **Fates reshuffled:** No one guaranteed game ending

## World Rules
- **Island:** Dozens miles across, held aloft by Core in Labyrinth. Upper surface meadows/cliffs, below honeycomb biomes. Cradle lit by Crack (seam overhead, morning when light hits Reservoir). Yamashita beds use polished slabs to scatter light
- **Red Flowers:** Strength/size/rage + loss of reason. Cooking dulls rage, not craving. Humans normally immune, Red Crystal/Eye bypasses immunity
- **Mugen:** Dream-being born from Ballos's leaking dreams (both 夢幻 dream/illusion and 無限 without limit). Lonely, envies Mimiga warmth, lures isolated/grieving, calls victims "petals". Touch with gem -> body becomes hollow Sleeper, soul drawn to endless dream. When Arthur sealed Mugen, link broke, Picked collapsed into unending sleep where they stood. Hozuki tends Hall of Sleepers. If seal breaks, Sleepers rise
- **Teleporters:** Ancient builder tech, mostly dead, Booster rewired some with plain English labels like "EGG CORRIDOR — STANDBY"

## Tone & Style Rules
- Show don't tell, environmental storytelling, sparse dialogue
- No edgy grimdark, no explicit content. Keep E10+ with dark undercurrents (genocide, slavery) handled honestly
- Mimiga dialogue: short, plain, present-focused, "Oh." "...", big thing said plainly then back to soup. Grief sideways. Humble titles
- Canon lines reused where scenes overlap, new lines matched to voice
- Quote never speaks aloud
- Each chapter opens with found text: sign, note, log, song (like game text boxes)
- Cozy, lonely, funny, sudden sorrow — Pixel's tone
- Flowers' adult properties (pairbloom aphrodisiac, sorrowcup addictive) treated frankly but never explicitly, giggled about away from children

## Prohibited
- Modern slang, 4th wall breaks, making Mimigas just mascots, gore
- Don't contradict this bible without updating changelog
