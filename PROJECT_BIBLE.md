## Core Premise
A year after Arthur's disappearance, Mimiga Village has collapsed from hundreds (thousands before war) to dozens in the Cradle. The Cradle — the game's camp but expanded with more Mimigas and repurposed spaces — is distinct from the vast, empty Old Village beyond it. When Sue Sakamoto, human-turned-Mimiga, falls into the Reservoir, Toroko shelters her in Arthur's house. The Doctor wants Sue back, but he also wants what's sealed beneath the graveyard: Mugen, and its Eye — called "gem" by some (user notes gem was mistake, but allow eye be called gem).

This is Mimiga-centric Cave Story: Quote sleeps through the opening the player remembers, so King, Jack, Toroko, and Sue must do what he did, and more. Fates unknown, will reveal as we write. Work opportunistically — outline can change if plotpoints/pathways reveal themselves during writing. Model may make up unverifiable / high-potential info or character interpretations to give historical events mysticism.
