## Overall Scope
- **Why 150k / 42 sections / 5 Petals?** Needs room for rooms described, meals eaten, silences sat in — novel not game. 5 petals from Mimiga oldest story: flower has five petals. Each Petal 8 chapters = progression Meal->Stems->Soul->Roots->Bloom (everyday to dangerous to deep past to gathering). 42 = Prologue+40+Epilogue, ~3500 avg, 16 installments of 2-3 chapters (~9-10k) substantial but not truncation risk.
- **Why start with plan + Prologue + Ch1-2?** User wants ambitious project, needs bible for consistency. First installment must feel like real launch not teaser, so ~2000-2500 plan + 1500-2000 prologue + 4000 each Ch1-2 = ~12k.

## Canon Grounding
- **Why research script/dialogue?** Author admits fuzzy memory on King/Toroko relationship, Jack warning, Mahin doctor mention, Chaco lipstick, Kanpachi Reservoir line, Santa Fireball, Momorin/Kazuma/Booster, Curly Sand Zone, Jenka puppies names, Ballos curse, Demon Crown gem, war lore. Doesn't want half-remembered canon, searches Aeon Genesis/Nicalis translations, wiki, Pixel BBS. Notes uncertainty, writes close variants not exact quotes where unsure.
- **Why keep six Mimigas?** Game opens with six left: King, Jack, Toroko, Mahin, Kanpachi, Sandaime. Canon anchor fans expect. King's "six of us left" line iconic.

## Village Split — Cradle vs Old Village
- **Why split?** User note: current Mimiga Village is small camp tucked in rocky cradle within larger dilapidated village sprawls outward overgrown trails abandoned homes liminal space good for exploration still hub by wanderers traders gatherers. Explains where 1000 lived, where they went, why empty but intact. Names Cradle (game's camp: Arthur's House teleporter, Assembly Hall, Shack, Reservoir, Yamashita Farm, Graveyard, Jack post, cage) vs Old Village (Terraces, Lantern Row, Old Square, Burrows). Allows market days, lamplighter, Hall of Sleepers. Reconciles King's six vs few dozen holdouts: Cradle 6 core + scattered holdouts refusing to leave.

## Mugen + Gem = Eye = Red Crystal
- **Why?** User's Mugen + gem not in canon, author puzzled what Mugen/gem correspond to. Recalls Demon Crown has red eye front, Red Crystal floating near Doctor, both unexplained origin. Unifies: crown broken in war 10y ago, eye knocked loose, fell deep, found by Mugen deep-dwelling creature, used to prey on isolated Mimigas at village edges transforming them. Explains Doctor's empty socket (touches when thinks no one watching) and motive to dig beneath village (seal under graveyard). Allows "picked up where he left off" — Mugen forced frenzy via gem alone, Doctor uses red flowers + crown. Misery knows crown history, tells Doctor Eye sealed in old village. Cleanest: gem = Red Crystal = Crown's lost Eye, Doctor mounted own synthetic red flower resin as substitute (partial power).

## Arthur Sealing Mugen
- **Why Arthur?** Arthur is deceased hero, grave and old house already established. User says Mugen sealed by someone before Doctor continued. Arthur perfect: warrior, Toroko's brother, Jack story Red Demon killed Mimigas Arthur defeated but didn't survive. Reinterpret: Red Demon = Mugen or its servant, Arthur didn't kill but sealed dying in process, Jack witnessed, buried empty grave, spread hero tale. Gives weight to key: Arthur gives Toroko brass key on cord "not even King" — not just house but teleporter + seal secret, King wants it for Sue handover. Toroko keeping key = honoring last request. Also explains Jack guarding graveyard (seal beneath) and mushrooms drawn to seal warmth.

## King's Red Eyes / Addiction
- **Why cooked?** Pixel BBS answer: "by red flower, for the power, He may have cooked it." Cooking reduces frenzy grants some strength but craving remains. King has secret ritual, pouch running low, ticking clock toward withdrawal, temper. Mirrors Arthur who ate red flowers to gain power to seal Mugen, became Red Demon theory — recontextualizes Toroko arc: brother not dead but transformed.

## Meal vs Soul Flowers
- **Why distinction?** User notes dedicated cultivation process. Need food economy: meal flowers fast hardy few calories once garnish now staple + fish. Soul flowers need flower-keeper ritual darkness timing water songs, marked by dark soul-line not just color (all red are soul but not all soul red). Allows varied effects: dreambell dream-walking funeral rites some never return, sorrowcup numbs grief habit-forming refugee towns, pairbloom pairing rite aphrodisiac giggled never front children, lullaby white bell cure costly scent restores reason. Children's rhyme: Sweet yellow salt blue lined not for you red never. Tasteful handling of aphrodisiac as lore courtship taboo not explicit.

## Voice & Style
- **Why short plain Mimiga dialogue?** Game scripts: blunt simple honesty Jack guard captain, Mimiga Chief warning soldier not hurt, Toroko simple earnestness. Use ellipses hesitation deadpan humor. Narration warm literary sensory caves light sound subtle music cues.
- **Why epigraphs?** Nostalgia — game text signs (Reservoir sign "fish are shy don't throw rocks", Santa house death trap). Each chapter opens found text.
- **Why Held in Reserve?** Tease mysteries not spoil: what's under Arthur's stone, why hands burnt honey, what Mugen calls picks, Igor old name Hamachi, where frenzied went Longears, what fills empty socket. Keep plan digestible.

## Structure Choices
- **Why prologue as folk tale?** Title Petals of a Flower needs meaning, Arthur telling Toroko flower story about lonely rock seed white 5-petal flower petals falling waking Mimigas "Oh. Hello. There you are." Flower didn't die became them — emotional core, establishes title, Arthur voice memory before death, nostalgic once upon time. Ends ominous night before sealing gives key.
- **Why Ch1 Kanpachi fishing Sue?** Kanpachi canon who says Sue fell in Reservoir, quiet perceptive grounded laconic voice suits entry point, echoes canon discovery. Second stool empty for Hamachi, green hair callback Sue human hair, something huge shifts below.
- **Why Ch2 Sue soup?** Sue POV modern sardonic surface girl scientifically literate mother, brave prickly scared, contrasts Mimiga cadence. Fragmented memories helicopter research camp Doctor kind then crown empty socket Misery Balrog Mom defiance transformation Misery hands light. Soup scene establishes meal flowers tastes salty sweet hot crisp, Toroko cheerful vs Sue hostility, locket photo.
