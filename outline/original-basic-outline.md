Cave Story: Petals of a Flower - Cursory notes.

This variation of Cave Story is a user crafted expansion of the original, aimed to flourish the narrative and depth of the characters, events, history, and vastness of the setting with non-canon info and suggestions. 

Events in this story match only the beginning events such as Sue's arrival, but diverge both in the past and future to allow for more varied character-centric storytelling and world building, with more character involvement and participation, and with ample space for pure exploration and character perspective.

Rough, disorganized notes: 
- Mimiga Village in its current form is a small part of a larger dilapidated village. The usual Mimiga cast live in a camp within the village pinched into a rocky cradle as it holds enough necessities for a small group to manage. The larger village is abandoned but largely intact, its empty trails and homes overgrown with weeds and vines. It's good for exploration and houses those who can stomach the large liminal emptiness, and wanderers/traders/gatherers who still treat it as a central hub. 
- Before the Doctor's arrival, another creature called Mugen used the gem to pick off and transform Mimiga. Mugen was sealed, but the Doctor came shortly after and picked up where he left off.
- After the war, Mimiga Village used to have hundreds of Mimiga nearing the thousands, with smaller settlements across the entire island. With the arrival and focus on the village, many have fled to other settlements and created new ones, while a numbered few stayed behind. There are a few dozen still occupying Mimiga Village. 
- The island is a very large expanse dozens of miles wide, with expansive and varied biomes and rich underground networks.
- Cultivated meal flowers are a varied low-calorie food item that grows quickly with minimal resources. Meal flowers were eaten for nutrients and spice, with vegetables making up the bulk of a Mimiga's diet plus added fruits, fish and meat being common, but with options strained, Mimigas in the camp live off mostly meal flowers and fish.
- Soul flowers require a dedicated process to cultivate, but they have a potent effect to the Mimiga's reactive biology, causing various metabolic, neurological, and mental effects depending on the flower, with some having hallucinogenic, addictive, and aphrodisiac properties. Red flowers are soul flowers.
- There are a vast multitude of creatures and communicative species within the island.
